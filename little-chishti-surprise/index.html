last night

You had a headache last night.
A small thing. You probably didn't think twice about mentioning it.

But after you went quiet, that small thing stayed with me.
I hoped you slept peacefully.
And somewhere in that quiet concern, I realised I had started caring a little more than I expected.

That’s the thing about slowly caring for someone.
You don’t always realise it immediately.
It just appears in small moments
like hoping they slept well, or wondering if they’re okay.

what I noticed

Your simplicity..
The confidence that doesn't need a room's attention.
How I find myself going to your profile when I open Instagram. Not looking for anything. Just looking.
And how your name, without asking for permission has started holding a small place in my day.
quietly beautiful
